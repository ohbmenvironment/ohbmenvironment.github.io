The SEA-SIG is hosting our annual social during the 2026 OHBM Annual Meeting in Bordeaux, France!

This year we have an especially exciting event planned! We have partnered with [Climate Fresk](https://climatefresk.org/world/) to put on a workshop for OHBMers where we can brainstorm together about the environmental impact of neuroimaging research activities, along with potential solutions (both individual + collective). Join together for an informal gathering with other scientists over food, drink, and engaging climate-related conversations!

The SEA-SIG Social will be taking place this year on the evening of <b>Tuesday, June 16th</b>! Location will be determined based on the estimated attendance.

RSVP [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScww5e8myF8vKhBkS6UAaZFVJqFo0fbi6gP1D68Q4eAGcdRCQ/viewform)!
